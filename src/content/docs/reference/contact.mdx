Python Central Hub is built and maintained by a small team, and we read every message. Whether you found a mistake in a lesson, want to suggest a new topic, or just need help, we would like to hear from you.

## How to reach us

- **Email**: [ravikishan63392@gmail.com](mailto:ravikishan63392@gmail.com)
- **Feedback form**: every lesson has a feedback form at the bottom. Use it for corrections, unclear explanations or exercise problems. It sends your note along with the page you were reading, which helps us fix things quickly.

## Before you write

- For a problem in a lesson, the feedback form on that lesson is the fastest route, since we automatically know which page you mean.
- For account, certificate or exam questions, email us and include the address you signed up with.
- Please do not send passwords. We will never ask for one.

We usually reply within a few days.
