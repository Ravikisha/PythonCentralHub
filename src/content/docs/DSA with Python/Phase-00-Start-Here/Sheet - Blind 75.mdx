Blind 75 came from a Meta engineer's post on Blind and is the ancestor of
every sheet since. It is deliberately small, which is both its strength and its
limit: 75 problems is achievable in two weeks, and 75 problems is not enough to
cover greedy, advanced graphs, or the harder DP shapes.

**Use it if** you are two weeks out, or you want a confidence pass over material
you already know. **Treat it as the minimum bar, not the target** -- clearing
Blind 75 does not mean you are ready for a Google onsite.

Note that it predates several problems now considered standard, so a few
categories look thin by modern standards. That is a property of the list, not a
transcription error.

## Track it here

Your progress is shared with every other list on this site -- tick a problem off
here and it is ticked off on its pattern page and in every other sheet that
includes it. That is the point of tracking them in one place.
