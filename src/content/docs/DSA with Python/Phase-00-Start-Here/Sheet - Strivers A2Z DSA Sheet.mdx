Striver's A2Z is the most complete free resource of the group, and the right
choice if you are genuinely starting from zero. It begins with language basics and
sorting -- material every other sheet assumes -- and only reaches interview-shaped
problems around step 9.

That completeness has a cost: 455 problems is a several-month commitment, and a
meaningful fraction of them are there for teaching rather than because they get
asked. Do not treat finishing it as a prerequisite for interviewing.

**Use it if** you have months rather than weeks, or if the other sheets have been
skipping over things you do not actually know yet.

## Track it here

Your progress is shared with every other list on this site -- tick a problem off
here and it is ticked off on its pattern page and in every other sheet that
includes it. That is the point of tracking them in one place.
