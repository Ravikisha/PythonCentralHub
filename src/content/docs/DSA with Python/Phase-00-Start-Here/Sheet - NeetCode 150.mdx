NeetCode 150 is the list most people mean when they say "the LeetCode list".
It extends Blind 75 with the coverage Blind 75 is thin on -- greedy, advanced
graphs, and a proper split between one- and two-dimensional DP -- and every
problem has a video solution, which is why it has largely displaced the original.

**Use it if** you already know what a hash map and a binary tree are, and you
want interview coverage rather than a CS course. **Do not** use it as your first
exposure to data structures; Striver's A2Z is the better start there.

The categories map almost one-to-one onto this course's phases, so you can work
the sheet and read the matching pattern page side by side.

## Track it here

Your progress is shared with every other list on this site -- tick a problem off
here and it is ticked off on its pattern page and in every other sheet that
includes it. That is the point of tracking them in one place.
