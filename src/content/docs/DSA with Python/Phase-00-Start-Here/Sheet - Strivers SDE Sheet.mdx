The SDE sheet is Striver's compressed list: 191 problems arranged as a 31-day
plan, assuming you already know the data structures. Days 28 to 31 cover operating
systems, DBMS, networks and system design, which are outside this course's scope
and appear here as empty steps.

**Use it if** you have already worked a full sheet and want a structured revision
pass with a deadline attached. The day boundaries are useful precisely because
they force you to stop.

## Track it here

Your progress is shared with every other list on this site -- tick a problem off
here and it is ticked off on its pattern page and in every other sheet that
includes it. That is the point of tracking them in one place.

<SheetTracker sheet="striver_sde" />

:::tip[Before you start this sheet]
Do the [consensus problems](../the-sheets-mapped/) first. They are the problems
every sheet author independently agreed on, so they are the highest-value
problems available regardless of which sheet you eventually commit to.
:::
